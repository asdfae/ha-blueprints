HOUSE LAYOUT - BASE GRID
========================

House type: suterränghus (Swedish). The house is on a slope.
The basement is partially below the ground.
Outer wall: 16 m (east-west) x 8 m (north-south).
Inner area: approximately 111 m2.
Scale: one cell (". ") is 0.5 m x 0.5 m. One metre is two cells.
View: the north side is at the bottom. The north side faces the viewer.
The viewer looks south. Thus, east is on the left and west is on the right.

LEVELS
------
All levels are relative to the main floor (0.0 m).
- Main floor: 0.0 m.
- Altan (south deck): 0.0 m, the same level as the main floor.
- Ground at the north entrance: approximately -0.9 m.
- Top of the basement: approximately 0.9 m above the ground at the north entrance.
- Basement floor: -2.4 m. The basement height is 2.4 m.

GROUND
------
The ground is low on the east side and high on the west side.
- East wall: approximately -2.4 m along the full wall. The east side of the basement is fully above the ground.
- South wall: -2.4 m at x 0 m. The ground goes up to 0.0 m at x 4 m.
  From x 4 m to x 9 m, the ground is at 0.0 m (the altan level).
- North wall: approximately -0.9 m at the entrance (x 6.5 m to 7.5 m).
- Not known (assumptions, correct them if necessary):
  - North wall from x 0 m to x 4 m: the ground goes up from -2.4 m to -0.9 m.
  - South wall from x 9 m to x 16 m: 0.0 m.
  - West wall: 0.0 m at the south corner, -0.9 m at the north corner.

HOW TO FILL IN THE GRID
-----------------------
1. Draw the inner walls. Use "|" for vertical walls and "-" for horizontal walls.
2. Put "+" where two walls meet.
3. Write the Home Assistant area name in each room (for example "Kitchen").
4. Mark each door with "D".
5. Mark each window with "W".
6. Mark the inside stairs with "S" on all cells of the stairs.
7. Do not change the outer wall.
8. Correct the position of the main entrance ("E") and the outside stairs ("s") if necessary.
9. If a floor does not exist, delete its grid.

LEGEND
------
+  corner or wall junction
|  vertical wall
-  horizontal wall
D  door
DDD  arched opening with a round top (no door). Three cells or more in a line.
d  French balcony door (no railing)
W  window
w  small window, high on the wall
S  inside stairs
E  main entrance
s  outside stairs
~  ground level (elevation only)
=  main floor level (elevation only)
.  empty cell (0.5 m x 0.5 m)
